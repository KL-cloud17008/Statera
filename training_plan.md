# Athanor Training Ledger - Scaled Strength and Mobility Plan

This is the documentation copy of the canonical training plan used by the app seed scripts and default workout constants.

User context for this phase: male, early 20s, 5 ft 9 in, about 312 lb (141.5 kg, 22 st 4 lb), detrained after months away, currently in week 2, day 3. Current work capacity is low and the feet/soles are already sensitive from recent walking volume, so the plan scales the Bryan Johnson / "You're Exercising Wrong" principles into a conservative injury-prevention block.

## Five Scaled Pillars

1. Strength: four gym days per week with machines, cables, dumbbells, supported free-weight work, controlled unilateral work, back-safe hinge alternatives, knee-friendly leg patterns, and shoulder-friendly pressing/pulling.
2. Low-intensity walking only: walking to the gym is the general warm-up, walking home is the low-intensity recovery walk, and no extra planned conditioning is added at this body weight and stage.
3. Mobility/flexibility: daily or near-daily low-fatigue work for feet, ankles, calves, shins, hips, thoracic spine, shoulders, knees, and lower back.
4. Balance: daily or near-daily supported balance work that improves control without chasing instability.
5. Recovery / injury prevention: Required later recovery is part of the training system, not extra work.

## Global Rules

- Global Daily Step Goal remains 8,000.
- Wednesday stays Mobility + 10,000 steps.
- If foot pain rises above 3/10, reduce next-day walking intensity or split steps into smaller chunks.
- Do not add machine-cardio warm-ups, interval conditioning, impact work, finishers, or extra conditioning circuits.
- No unstable surfaces and no eyes-closed balance drills in this phase.
- Use RPE 5-7 for most work with 2-4 reps in reserve.
- No failure training, max holds, jumping, or high-impact drills.
- Workout loads are logged in kg.
- Bodyweight is tracked in lb with kg and stone conversion.

## Workout Day Format

1. At home - mobility primer.
2. Walk to gym - general warm-up.
3. At gym - 1-2 ramp-up sets on the first programmed lift or machine.
4. Main strength blocks.
5. Required later recovery - done later the same day, not necessarily immediately after training.

At the gym:

- Do not add any machine-cardio warm-up; walking to the gym already covers the general warm-up.
- Do 1-2 easy ramp-up sets on the first programmed lift or machine of Block A.
- Ramp-up sets should feel easy, around RPE 3-5.
- Then begin the first working block.

Required later recovery does not have to be done immediately after training. Complete it later the same day after walking home, food, shower, or before bed. It is part of the training system, not extra work.

Required does not mean push through pain. Stop or scale down if you feel sharp pain, numbness, tingling, swelling, warmth, limping, dizziness, or pain that increases as you continue.

If feet/soles flare, choose required foot-flare recovery. Foot flare recovery is required when your soles are irritated or recent step load is high. The goal is to reduce guarding, restore easy motion, and protect tomorrow's training.

## Daily Lower-Leg and Balance Base

Daily minimum: 6-8 minutes.

| Exercise | Dose | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seated Ankle Pumps | 15-20 reps/side | Gentle ankle motion and lower-leg circulation |
| Ankle Circles | 1-2 sets of 5-10 slow circles each direction per ankle | Ankle awareness and comfortable joint motion |
| Wall Ankle Rocks | 8-10 reps/side | Dorsiflexion for walking and leg press setup |
| Wall Calf Stretch - Knee Straight | 20-30 sec/side | Calf mobility without forcing the ankle |
| Wall Calf Stretch - Knee Bent | 20-30 sec/side | Soleus and Achilles-area mobility |
| Supported Tandem Balance Hold | 2 rounds x 20-40 seconds each stance | Foot-ankle control, knee tracking, and hip stability |
| Supported Single-Leg Balance with Toe-Touch Kickstand | 2 rounds x 10-30 seconds per side | Single-leg control, plantar-fascia resilience, and ankle stiffness |

## Progression Logic

Strength:

- Use double progression.
- When all sets reach the top of the rep range at target RPE with clean form, add the smallest available load next time.
- If RPE exceeds 7, form degrades, or joint pain appears, keep the load the same or reduce.

Balance:

- Progress support first, not instability.
- Wider stance to narrower stance.
- More hand support to less hand support.
- 10 seconds to 20 seconds to 30 seconds to 40 seconds.
- Do not progress if foot pain, knee wobble, or arch collapse increases.

Mobility/flexibility:

- Progress by smoother range, better control, and lower pain.
- Do not chase extreme range.
- Pain stays 0-2/10.

## Pain and Safety Rules

- 0-2/10 mild discomfort: acceptable if it does not worsen.
- 3-4/10 discomfort: regress range, load, stance, or duration.
- 5/10+ pain: stop that exercise.
- Sharp pain: stop immediately.
- Numbness or tingling: stop.
- Swelling, warmth, or limping: end lower-body loading and switch to recovery.
- Plantar-fascia pain increasing during walking: reduce walking load and do the recovery block.
- Next-day foot pain worse than baseline: remove Tuesday lunges or the Tuesday wall sit temporarily and keep machine work.
- Knee pain during lunges: shorten range, use support, or replace with a supported split-stance hold.
- Back pain during back extension: shorten range or skip.
- Shoulder pinch during pressing or raises: reduce range, switch to a machine, or use a neutral grip.

## Tuesday - Lower A

Lunges / Walking Lunges beginner note: use bodyweight first, rest 3-5 minutes, stop if feet/knees/balance do not tolerate it, and do not treat the movement as conditioning.

Wall Sit beginner note: back supported against the wall, feet flat, knees tracking over the middle toes, and use a high wall-sit angle rather than a deep position. Breathe steadily, stop before leg shaking becomes excessive, keep pain 0-2/10 maximum, and skip if knees, feet, or lower back are irritated that day. This is not conditioning and not a max hold.

Lower A at-home primer: daily lower-leg and balance base; 90/90 hip switches, 6-8 slow reps; half-kneeling or standing hip flexor stretch, 20-30 sec per side; adductor rock-backs, 6-8 reps per side; bodyweight glute bridge, 8-10 controlled reps; seated bracing breaths, 5 slow breaths.
